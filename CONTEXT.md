# CONTEXT.md — PGSR / sherd surfacing

Single-context glossary for this repo. Cross-project measurement terms live in `../docs/glossary.md` and outrank anything here.

| term | what it means here |
|---|---|
| **edge** | Break-face relief in millimetres (glossary Ra/Rq height after the break's own meander is filtered out, cutoff stated), measured on the break-face ribbon. Not rim silhouette sharpness, not render crispness. R1 scores this and nothing else. |
